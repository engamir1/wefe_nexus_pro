밄 Lecture 1: Identification & Classification  
Objective:  Establishing the "Nexus Logic" to attract premium climate financing.  
 Theory of Change (ToC):  Used to map systemic feedback loops (e.g., how water 
efficiency leads to energy savings).  
o 꼏꼐 Guide: UNDP Theory of Change Guidelines  
 WEFE Nexus Screening Matrix:  A tool to check if a project is truly "integrated" or

o 꼏꼐 Guide: FAO WEF Nexus Rapid Appraisal Manual  
 Real-World Case:  The Noor Ouarzazate Solar Complex (Morocco).  This CSP plant

o 꼏꼐 Reference:  World Bank Project Portal: Noor Ouarzazate  
 
밃 Lecture 2: Integrating WEFE into Existing Projects  
Objective:  Identifying "entry points" for optimization to reduce OPEX.  
 Problem & Objectives Tree:  Finding the root causes of resource waste (e.g., high 
energy bills due to water leaks).  
o 꼏꼐 Guide: EuropeAid Project Cycle Management Guidelines  
 Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis (MCDA):  Prioritizing which retrofit (e.g., biogas vs. 
solar pumps) has the best ROI.  
o 꼏꼐 Guide: OECD Multi-Criteria Analysis in Infrastructure Governance  
 Real-World Case:  Johannesburg Wastewater-to-Energy.  Retrofitting plants with 
anaerobic digesters to produce biogas (Energy) from sludge (Waste).  
o 꼏꼐 Reference:  WRC South Africa: Energy from Wastewater Report

 Incremental Cost Analysis:  Proving why the "Nexus" solution is better than the

꾂 Lecture 4: Financing Risks During Operations 
Objective:  Ensuring the project remains solvent during droughts or market shifts. 
 Climate Risk & Vulnerability Assessment (CRVA):  Stress-testing the project against 
2050 climate scenarios. 
o 꼏꼐 Guide: World Bank Climate & Disaster Risk Screening Tools  
 Financial Stress Test (DSCR/NPV):  Calculating if the project can pay its debt if 
electricity costs rise or crop yields drop. 
o 꼏꼐 Guide: World Bank Infrastructure Finance & PPP Toolkit  
 Real-World Case:  Floating Solar on Hydropower Reservoirs.  Solar protects water 
from evaporating while producing power when water is low. 
o 꼏꼐 Reference:  World Bank: Floating Solar Panels - New Renewable Frontier
